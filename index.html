Our Mission

A Peer Network for Healthier Choices

Empowering students to build healthier, drug-free communities through leadership, education, and prevention.

NSPN develops highly informative case studies on substance use and delivers them to high schools nationwide, equipping students with accurate, evidence-based information and opening honest conversations about substance abuse.

Prevention First

We build every workshop and campaign from current research — not scare tactics or outdated slogans.

Peer-to-Peer Trust

College mentors train high school leaders. High school leaders run the room — student to student, not adult to teenager.

Nationally Connected

What works in one chapter gets documented and shared with every other chapter, coast to coast.

Where NSPN Started

NSPN started with a simple observation: most substance-prevention programs are designed by adults, for teenagers, and rarely land the way they’re supposed to. We flip that model. College chapters research the science, then work directly with high school chapter leaders to turn it into case studies and conversations that actually resonate with their peers. What began as a handful of campus chapters sharing curriculum with nearby high schools has grown into a nationwide network built on one idea: prevention works better when it comes from someone you trust.

SAMHSA Strategic Prevention Framework Trained

Every NSPN board member completes training in SAMHSA’s Strategic Prevention Framework (SPF) — the five-step, evidence-based planning process (assessment, capacity, planning, implementation, and evaluation) that the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration developed to guide prevention work nationwide. Completing SPF training is a requirement for every incoming board member, so every case study and campaign NSPN produces is grounded in the same standard used by prevention professionals across the country.

What We Do

Everything We Run Starts With the Research

Curriculum and campaigns are only as good as the science behind them. Ours comes from published addiction research — not assumptions.

Evidence-Based Curriculum

Workshops are rebuilt every year to reflect current neuroscience and public health findings on substance use.

Peer Counseling

Trained student leaders offer confidential, judgment-free conversations — no parents or teachers in the room.

Community Campaigns

Chapters run their own awareness weeks, guest lectures, and outreach events on their own campus calendar.

Research & Advocacy

Findings from every chapter are presented each year at NSPN’s National Research Symposium and shared with policymakers.
